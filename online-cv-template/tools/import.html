Import from your LinkedIn® data export

Your files stay on this computer. This page reads them in your browser and uploads nothing.

1. Get your export

  1. On LinkedIn, open Me › Settings & Privacy › Data privacy › Download your data.
  2. Choose the larger archive, or tick Positions, Education, Profile, Skills, Certifications and Recommendations, then Request archive.
  3. LinkedIn emails you a link, usually within a day. Download the .zip.

2. Drop it here

3. Check what was read

4. Save the updated site files

The download holds cv.json and every page with a marked region, already rewritten. Replace the files in your site folder with these, then edit any wording by hand. Then rebuild cv.pdf and cv-full.pdf: run node tools/build-pdf.mjs, or open print.html?full=0 and ?full=1, use Print, Save as PDF, and save them in assets/.
