Open the shop

Hello, I’m Mariam.

I make stoneware in a small studio off Northdown Road: thrown mugs with handles you can get four fingers through, wonky jugs, and plates for The Penny Whelk and two other kitchens on the coast.

The kiln goes on about every six weeks. Whatever comes out goes on sale that Saturday, seconds and all.

Studio 4, Northdown Road, Cliftonville, Margate. Visits by appointment.
