Expedition note / north face

Keep a line
through the noise.

A short instruction for moving with intent when the terrain refuses certainty.

01 — Observe

Read the weather before the map.

Plans are clean. Ground is not. Note the wind, the loose stone and the quiet change in light. Precision begins by looking again.

02 — Traverse

Choose the useful line.

The shortest route is rarely the truest. Move across the slope, keep three points steady, and let each deliberate step redraw the way forward.

03 — Return

Finish at the mark.

Carry back only what changed your judgement. A route is complete when the final coordinate is exact—and reversible all the way home.
