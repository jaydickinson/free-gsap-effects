Frequently asked questions

Billing, plans and seats. For anything else, contact our support team.

Your card is charged on the day you subscribe, then on the same date each month or year depending on your plan. If that date does not exist in a given month, such as the 31st, we charge on the last day of that month instead.

Yes, from Billing in your account settings. What happens next depends on the direction:

  • Monthly to annual takes effect straight away, and the unused part of your current month is credited against the annual price.
  • Annual to monthly keeps your annual price until the paid year ends, then moves you to monthly billing on the renewal date.
  • Either way, every invoice stays in one history under Billing.

Admins can change the seat count at any time under Members. Added seats are billed straight away at a prorated rate for the rest of the period. Removed seats stay usable until the period ends and come off your next invoice.

We retry the card three times over seven days and email your billing contact after each attempt. Your workspace keeps working normally in the meantime.

If the last retry fails, the account becomes read-only: everyone can still sign in and export their data, but nothing new can be created until the balance is paid. Updating the card under Billing clears it immediately.

Cancel from Billing whenever you like; there is no notice period. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for and are not charged again. If you cancelled an annual plan recently, check whether you can get a refund.

Annual plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase or renewal are refunded in full to the original payment method. Monthly plans are not refunded.
