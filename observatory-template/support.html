Membership and support

No grant keeps a 200-tonne dish turning. Members and volunteers do.

The Trust bought the station for a pound in 2019. Everything since, from the bearings to the planetarium seats, has been paid for by people who visit and came back.

Volunteer

Bring a skill, or learn one

Most of our 140 volunteers had never touched a telescope. Work parties are on Saturdays; training is free.

SaturdaysDish crew

Painting, greasing, panel surveys. Head for heights and a harness course provided.

EveningsObserving team

Run a telescope on Friday or Saturday nights. We teach you the sky first.

DaytimeGuides and gate

Tours of the control room, tickets, and the questions children ask about toilets in space.
