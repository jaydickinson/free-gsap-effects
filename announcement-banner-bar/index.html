Delivery and returns

Orders placed before 3pm on a working day are picked the same afternoon. Anything after that goes out the next morning. You get a tracking link as soon as the parcel is scanned, and a second message when it is out for delivery.

Delivery times

Standard delivery is two to four working days. Express is next working day if the order is placed before 3pm. Remote postcodes can take an extra day, and we say so at checkout rather than after you have paid.

If a parcel has not moved for three days, reply to the dispatch email and we will open a claim with the carrier. You do not need to chase it yourself.

Returns

You have 30 days from delivery to send anything back, for any reason. Items should be unworn and in their original packaging, with the tags still attached. Print the label from your order page, or ask for one to be posted to you.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five working days of the return arriving. Exchanges are shipped as soon as the return is scanned, so you are not waiting twice.

Duties and taxes

Prices include duties for every country we ship to, so there is nothing to pay on arrival. If a carrier asks you for a fee anyway, send us the paperwork and we will refund it.

VAT is shown separately on your invoice. Business customers can add a VAT number to their account and it will appear on every invoice from then on.

Changing an order

An order can be changed or cancelled until it is picked, which is usually within a couple of hours. After that, the fastest route is to accept the delivery and return it, which keeps the refund on the same timeline as everything else.
